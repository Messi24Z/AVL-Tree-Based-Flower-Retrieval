# project2


As a newly hired member of a startup company specializing in biochemical processes, you have been assigned a critical project in the field of wound healing. The company is focusing on the application of Polyphenol Oxidation, utilizing tiger lilies and sunflowers, to accelerate the healing process. The objective is to develop a system that can quickly locate, retrieve, add, and modify specific flowers within the system. The operations must be performed as rapidly as possible since the wound sites are chemically monitored and require unpredictable and specific flower attributes to maintain the desired chemical properties.

Project Tasks:
1. Design an AVL (Adelson-Velsky and Landis) tree data structure to efficiently manage and organize the tiger lilies and sunflowers within the system. The AVL tree will enable fast searching, insertion, deletion, and modification of flower data.

2. Develop functionality to locate and retrieve flowers with specific attributes based on the chemical properties required for wound healing. This capability will enable the system to identify and provide the next flower quickly, ensuring seamless and continuous application of Polyphenol Oxidation.

3. Perform rigorous testing and debugging to ensure the reliability, efficiency, and accuracy of the flower retrieval system. Test various scenarios to validate the performance and functionality of the AVL tree-based operations.

4. Document the project, including the design choices, implementation details, and user instructions. Create a future recommendations for enhancements.

This project assignment will allow you to demonstrate your understanding of AVL trees in a hypothetical area of research - wound healing using Polyphenol Oxidation. By creating an efficient flower retrieval system, you will contribute to the company's mission of advancing biochemical processes and potentially making significant advancements in the field of accelerated wound healing.
